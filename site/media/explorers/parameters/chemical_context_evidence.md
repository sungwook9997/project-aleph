## Source cards

**C01 — Exact BioNumbers identity.** [BNID 101266](https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?id=101266) reports rat-liver G1P, 16 µM. Its reference is Albe, Butler & Wright 1990, with Williamson & Brosnan's tissue-metabolite compilation as the underlying source. The separate [BNID 101256](https://bionumbers.hms.harvard.edu/bionumber.aspx?id=101256) reports rat-liver ATP, 3535 µM. Neither record is a matched MCF7 free-pool observation. The second identifier is a candidate to inspect, not an automatic correction; original volume and assay definitions remain missing.

**C02 — Declared Milo & Phillips pointer.** The [ATP hydrolysis chapter](https://book.bionumbers.org/how-much-energy-is-released-in-atp-hydrolysis/) distinguishes actual and standard-state energy, and states that organism, compartment and energetic demand matter. Its table recalculates values using a chosen transformed standard value and explicitly assumes one missing phosphate concentration. This is a secondary explanatory source, not proof that the current generic energy belongs to the prepared 3 mM ATP / 0.1 mM ADP / 1 mM Pi combination.

**C03 — Calibrated intracellular ATP.** [Yoshida et al. 2016](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep39618), Figure 4 and Methods, use a purified BTeam calibration at 37 °C to infer cellular ATP from a BRET ratio. The authors operationally call their nucleotide quantity Mg-ATP. HeLa cytosolic ATP is reported as 3.8 ± 0.1 mM, with SD; this is not MCF7 or the model pool. The separate lysate assay uses ATP amount divided by cell count and volume estimated from trypsinized-cell diameters. The sensor's Hill K0.5 is not a Mg-ATP dissociation constant. Figure 4 was visually inspected; its replicate-count distinctions are retained in JSON.

**C04 — MCF7 ATP production is a flux.** [Louie et al. 2020](https://doi.org/10.3389/fonc.2020.01703) convert oxygen and extracellular proton fluxes to ATP production, in pmol ATP/min/µg protein, using stoichiometric assumptions. The 37 °C assay uses extracellular KRPH at pH 7.4 and 1 mM added MgCl₂. These are assay conditions, not cytosolic pH or free Mg measurements. The 180 µL well volume is not cytosolic aqueous volume. No ATP concentration prior follows from the production rate.

**C05 — MCF7 compartmental pH.** [Altan et al. 1998](https://doi.org/10.1084/jem.187.10.1583), Table 1 and Figure 6, report parental MCF-7 cytosol pH 6.75 ± 0.3 and nucleoplasm pH 7.1 ± 0.1. SNARF-dextrans distinguish compartments; in-situ ionophore calibration is described. Imaging was at 37 °C, with extracellular pH 7.3, after scrape loading and replating. The table's sample size and definition of ± were not established. The result is a protocol-specific comparison, not a replacement for 7.2 or a matched suspended-cell result. No isogenic claim about the historical MCF-7/ADR comparator is used.

**C06 — Primary conditional MgATP affinity.** [Chinopoulos et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2008.12.3915), [Supporting Material](https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0006349509003798-mmc1.pdf), printed p. 1, Eq. 2 and Fig. S1B/C, fit separate ADP and ATP titrations at pH 7.25 and 37 °C. Starting total Mg is 1 mM; MgGreen estimates free Mg. Reported apparent Kd values are 0.114 ± 0.005 mM for ATP and 0.906 ± 0.023 mM for ADP. Main-text Statistics gives a global mean ± SE convention. The fit paragraph does not resolve fit-parameter SE versus between-experiment SE or identify this fit’s replicate n; titration boluses are not independent samples. The medium is explicitly defined, including K-gluconate and phosphate; it is not a measurement of cytosolic ionic strength. The fit page was visually checked. This nearby number does not establish the provenance or transferability of current 0.1 mM.

**C07 — MCF7 assay reuses C06.** [Chen et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.15252/embj.2022112094), Figure 7B and Methods, assess shGFP/shATM MCF7 cells after trypsinization and digitonin permeabilization. MgGreen fluorescence in a 200 µL reaction is converted to extramitochondrial ATP following ADP addition. The paper imports earlier Kd values; [Kawamata et al. 2010](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ab.2010.07.031) is an intervening method adaptation, not a new calibration supporting the current parameter. Mg and derived ATP are one observation chain, not two independent targets. Figure source data are advertised, but were not downloaded; the paper declares no external repository deposit. Assay temperature and the equation's time-zero composition require clarification before parsing.

**C08 — Thermodynamic quantity conventions.** [Vinnakota et al. 2009](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0076-6879(08)03802-0), Sections 2.3 and 3.2.1, distinguish reference chemical species from biochemical sums across ion-bound forms. The Terminology table specifies a 1 M reference concentration. Apparent equilibrium constants and transformed energies depend on the stated ion conditions. The cardiomyocyte example is a conditional calculation. This methods chapter is not a joint MCF7 observation, and none of its constants or models is adopted here.

**C09 — Water permittivity and temperature.** [Malmberg & Maryott 1956](https://doi.org/10.6028/jres.056.001), [official NIST PDF](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/jres/56/jresv56n1p1_A1b.pdf), Table 2, printed p. 6: 74.828 at 35 °C and 73.151 at 40 °C. These are values of the empirical fit, with fit-minus-observation residuals in a separate column. The stated ±0.05 overall accuracy is not SD/SEM. The table was visually checked. No 37 °C replacement is calculated, and pure-water permittivity is not relabeled as effective crowded-cytosol permittivity.

**C10 — ADP inference versus tissue extraction.** [Veech et al. 1979](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36399/) compare tissue metabolite contents with enzyme-equilibrium quantities. The primary abstract distinguishes measured total tissue ADP from inferred free cytosolic ADP, under explicit ion and temperature conditions. Only the abstract was obtained; original tables, volume normalization and uncertainty remain uninspected. It supports the quantity distinction, not current 100 µM ADP or 1000 µM Pi.

**C11 — Definitions, not cell observations.** Official IUPAC entries define [activity relative to its standard state](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/A00115), [pH by hydrogen-ion activity](https://old.goldbook.iupac.org/html/P/P04524.html), and [Debye length through the charge-squared concentration sum](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/T06343/plain). These were inspected through official indexed content; failed direct retrievals remain recorded. With concentration-based `I = ½ Σ cᵢzᵢ²`, the latter expression gives the declared `2I` form. A symmetric monovalent salt is an example, not a required identity condition. This does not validate the approximation in a cell.

## Relationships that remain conditional

1. **Energy anchor.** Frozen `nucleotide_composition.py:205–243` treats `delta_g_atp` as the positive energy released at the prepared ATP/ADP/Pi composition. It applies the ratio of live and prepared concentration quotients. Dimensions cancel in that ratio when units agree; no missing-1-M arithmetic defect is asserted. The unresolved issue is whether the baseline energy was established at that preparation, with consistent activities, species and ion conditions. Its kBT comes from `bonds.py:70–72`, which reads `cell.temperature`; this is distinct from screening’s temperature selection below.
2. **MgATP mapping.** Frozen `electrolyte.py:128–173` uses `Mg_free / (Kd + Mg_free)` under buffered free Mg and a single-site convention. Its ATP denominator is the available aqueous pool across represented forms, excluding molecule-bound nucleotide. A BTeam Mg-ATP estimate or a total-cell extract cannot enter that denominator without a quantity mapping. No alternate binding model is proposed.
3. **Screening.** `electrolyte.py:107` selects positive `osmotic.reference_temperature` before `cell.temperature`; both are 310 K in this frozen input. Debye length, ionic strength, permittivity and temperature form a conditional consistency relation, not four independent empirical observations. The solvent-property source still needs verification.
4. **Shared observations.** Calibrated MgGreen and ATP computed from that signal must retain one data lineage. Citation reuse of a Kd and reuse of a calibration do not create independent source support. No current same-fit group or covariance is added.
