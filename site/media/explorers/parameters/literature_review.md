## Resolved tentative concerns

- **nie_numeric_count_resolved**: Official primary full text explicitly derives N=40±20 from MRLC-GFP foci and regional intensity for control TypeII HeLa. Numeric0.625 is supported; method label super-resolution remains conflicting.
- **pollard_egta_resolved**: PrimaryTable I/abstract use1mM;Fig5 caption explicitly uses1.1mM. This is section-dependent metadata, not a simple unsupported1.1mM claim.
- **geometry_ratio_convention_resolved**: 2016 Eq10 defines nuclear cross-sectional area divided by cytoplasmic cross-sectional area.2019 uses nucleus/cell diameter ratio. Do not pool these definitions.

## Dependencies and fit families

- `cell.radius` derives from source volume andspherical-equivalence assumption.
- `cell.nc_ratio` must retain same-cohort nucleus/cell measurement and ratio convention.
- `material.actin.EA` derives from stiffness times measured filament length; `material.actin.kappa` derives from persistence length and temperature.
- IF `EA` andbending rigidity derived from `E` andradius add a constitutive assumption.
- `envelope.area_modulus` adds a two-bilayer parallel assumption to the single-bilayer parent observation.
- Both NMII `x_adp` rows depend on rate ratios, assumed load and temperature; characteristic force is a derived child, not independent evidence.
- ERM rate andbarrier share `Braunger2014_EvansRitchie_Fig8_ezrinT567D`; crosslink rate andbarrier share `Ferrer2008_HummerSzabo_alphaActinin_p9224`. No covariance is invented.
- NMII density derives from fluorescence intensity andentity interpretation.
- `cortex.seg` and`membrane.mesh` bind to `resolution.spacing`, so they are not independent observed labels.
- Osmotic conversion derives from gas constant, reference temperature andunit conversion.
