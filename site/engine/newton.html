Physics engine / Time step

Implicit step

Each time step is an implicit solve of the whole world's energy with Newton iterations and a line search. A step that stops at its iteration budget is recorded as FORCE-ACCEPTED; the native runs on this site are all in that class.

Laws

This part has no law card of its own; it is the machinery the laws run on.

Where it lives in the code

  • aleph/physics/implicit.py
  • aleph/physics/integrate.py
  • aleph/physics/predict.py
  • aleph/physics/newton_row_merit.py
  • aleph/physics/diagnostics.py
