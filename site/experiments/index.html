Parallel plates

30 September · preview

What the instrument recorded

The plate was driven 1.0 µm in at 10 µm/s (steps 5–15), held (to step 80) and released (steps 80–90). The force on it peaked at 3,567 pN at the end of the approach and relaxed to 2,261 pN over the hold.

Apparent tension · 1,630 pN/µm

Inferred with the instrument’s formula. This is a model preview, not a physical result.

Interpretation, source and run conditions

The plate's formula (Laplace's law for a drop, in the form of Fischer-Friedrich et al. 2014) turns that force and the contact size (contact radius 1.15 µm, cell radius 4.12 µm) into an apparent tension of 1,630 pN/µm. The cortex's own forces read 41.8 pN/µm under load (step 25) and 12.7 pN/µm at step 100, after the release: a 39–128× gap.

The same virtual cell: the instrument's formula and the cortex disagree 39- to 128-fold on this build. Earlier build, with the kick: 1,640 against 53.8 pN/µm (31–33×).

Evidence, from the pack: FORCE-ACCEPTED RUN, 3 Newton/step (median); residual median 3.52e3 pN (step-end net force, 20 saved steps); contact share 0.99; worst step 11 (net force 9,607 pN, up from 6,869 pN).

γ_CMC = F (b² − a²) / (2π b a²) with F 3,567 pN, a 1.15 µm, b 4.12 µm (step 15): the band-selected contact radius and the largest lateral membrane radius, not a fitted shape. Cortex tension: global estimator over the full shell (cortex, Arp2/3 cortex, myosin II), 41.8 / 17.1 / 12.7 pN/µm at steps 25 / 50 / 100.

Penalty depth: the pack's caveat reads nodes up to 71 nm inside at its raw frames (step 25); over every saved frame, membrane nodes reach 88 nm past the plate's face at step 15. Not contact-protected.

AFM sphere (R 2.5 µm)

30 September · preview

What the instrument recorded

The sphere was driven in at 5 µm/s (steps 10–40), held (to step 70) and pulled back. The force reached 7,568 pN at 0.128 µm past contact at the end of the approach; the sphere crept to 0.23 µm while held.

Apparent modulus E* · 81.7 kPa

Inferred with the instrument’s formula. This is a model preview, not a physical result.

Interpretation, source and run conditions

Hertz's law for a sphere on a half-space, fitted to the approach frames the pack declares (four frames, from contact to the end of the approach), reads an apparent modulus E* = 81.7 ± 5.5 kPa (E = 61.3 ± 4.1 kPa with ν = 0.5 assumed). That is about 16× the earlier build's 5.0 kPa. The build changed the cell (in this smaller cell, cytoplasmic actin went from 87,494 to 185,935 nodes and myosin II from 20,828 to 30,988, and the new branch law holds every Arp2/3 junction at the full bending stiffness), and T and the Newton budget changed too, so the cause is not isolated.

The probe is a penalty face (k 1000 pN/µm) on the membrane's nodes only, not contact-protected: membrane nodes sit up to 91 nm inside the sphere (step 40), and cortex nodes, which it does not press, up to 180 nm (step 75).

Evidence, from the pack: FORCE-ACCEPTED RUN, 3 Newton/step (median); residual median 3.26e3 pN (20 saved steps); contact share 0.99; worst step 6 (net force 7,371 pN, up from 3,570 pN); frame audit VALID (containment and exact membrane parity at steps 0, 40, 70 and 100).

Missing physics, as the pack lists it: no excluded volume between filaments (the cortex's steric resistance is absent from the force); fluid off (node drag only).

Earlier build, with the kick: E* 4,992 ± 859 Pa (record bead_deep_ka_v2_mini_20260928_001_4fc2981d4). What differs between the two records: the node counts above (the Arp2/3 cortex is 773,859 nodes in both), the bending triples on the Arp2/3 network (515,906 → 773,859), and 25 rows of the parameter file (among them crosslinker and filamin binding rates that were zero).

No tension reading: the bead–membrane bond let go mid-pull, so the readout withholds σ (after the release the force is not a tether force).

Interpretation, source and run conditions

The trap moved at 1 µm/s. Micropipette aspirationNo instrument reading

Nothing was drawn in: the aspirated length is 0 at every recorded step of the ramp, from 20 to 300 Pa.

Interpretation, source and run conditions

The stored flow shows why. The cell sits 0.51 µm short of the pipette's mouth at every recorded step, so the pipette pumps bath fluid: up to 1.05 × 10⁴ µm/s in the bore, a Reynolds number of about 0.045, so the flow is laminar as it must be. Only 74 of the 300 Pa acts at the mouth; the rest of the pressure drop is spent along the bore. Streamlines cross the membrane: the continuity share is 0.052 against the 0.002 bar. The protocol has no seal step.

Earlier build, with the kick: also nothing drawn in.

Evidence, from the score file: FORCE-ACCEPTED (3 Newton/step); residual median 3.47e3 pN (30 steps); contact share 1.00. The pre-registered check M1 reads: falsified, L < R_p at every recorded step up to the ramp's top.

Peak speed 1.045 × 10⁴ µm/s is the largest face velocity of the stored grid at step 30; the Reynolds number uses the bore diameter (3 µm), the fluid density row (1 pg/µm³) and η 0.692 mPa·s. The gap to the mouth is the largest membrane-node position along the pipette axis, measured from the saved frames; the continuity share is the score file's (above the bar at all 15 recorded steps).
